WHAT WE DO

Support for every
next step.

From personal returns to small business tax support, we're here to help you understand what comes next.

01 / INDIVIDUALS & FAMILIES

Tax return
preparation

Preparation support for federal and New York State individual returns, with attention to your income, family situation, and eligible credits.

  • W-2 and other income
  • Dependents and family-related credits
  • Education and childcare items, when eligible
02 / SELF-EMPLOYED & SMALL BUSINESS

Small business
tax support

Help organizing tax information and preparing returns for self-employed people and small businesses. Filing requirements depend on your business structure.

  • Business income and expense review
  • Schedule C support, when applicable
  • Year-round recordkeeping guidance
03 / YEAR-ROUND CARE

Amended returns
& IRS letters

Need to correct a previous return or understand a tax notice? Get help reviewing next steps and organizing relevant information.

  • Prior-year filing support
  • Amended return preparation review
  • Notice and document organization
